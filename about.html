About Mind Blossoms

Psychological care rooted in resilience.

Mind Blossoms is a professional psychological practice dedicated to nurturing resilience and empowering individuals to thrive. We provide a safe, trauma-informed environment for adolescents, young adults, and adults to navigate life transitions, explore challenges, and develop practical coping strategies.

Meet the psychologist

Registered & Licensed Psychologist, CPB Kenya. Vanessa brings an empathetic, client-centred, and practical approach to care — focused on building resilience and emotional safety, whether she's on a video call with an individual client or on-site with a school community.

What Mind Blossoms stands for

Two disciplines, one philosophy of care

Confidentiality first

Every individual session and every school consultation is held to the same standard of discretion and professional ethics.

Meeting people as they are

Care is shaped around the person or the institution in front of us — not a one-size-fits-all script.

Growth that lasts

The goal is never a single good session — it's tools, language, and habits that keep supporting you afterward.
