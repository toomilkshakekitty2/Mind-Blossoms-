100% Virtual · Individual Therapy

Confidential therapy, from wherever you feel safe.

Structured online sessions delivered via secure video call. 100% virtual — no in-person therapy is offered.

For clients under 18

For clients under 18, parental/guardian consent is required. We will contact parents/guardians to complete a consent form before the first session.

Who it's for

Adolescents, young adults, and adults

Individual therapy is for adolescents (ages 13+, with required parental/guardian consent and involvement), young adults, and adults.

Common reasons people seek support

Stress, anxiety & low mood

Practical, evidence-based tools for stress, anxiety, and depression-related concerns.

Life transitions & guidance

Academic pressure, career changes, or big life shifts — a space to think clearly about what's next.

Self-esteem & regulation

Building a steadier relationship with yourself and your emotions, at a pace that fits you.

Caregiver & family support

Support for the people holding everyone else together — parents, caregivers, and families in transition.

Session details

What to expect

  • Session duration: 50 minutes
  • Delivery method: Google Meet or Zoom — a secure video link is provided
  • Tailored care: services are shaped to your needs following an initial assessment

Payment & cancellation

Good to know before you book

  • Payment is required before session commencement
  • M-Pesa and bank transfer are accepted payment methods
  • 24-hour cancellation notice is required; cancellations within 24 hours are subject to a 50% session fee

Confidentiality

All information shared in therapy sessions is confidential and protected by law, except in cases of imminent danger, child protection concerns, or court-ordered disclosure.
